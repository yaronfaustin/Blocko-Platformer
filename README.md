# Blocko Platformer

A 2D platformer independently developed using GameMaker and GameMaker Language (GML).

Originally released in 2018, Blocko features 33 levels across three worlds, combining challenging level design, varied gameplay mechanics and a range of power-ups.

## Features

- **33 levels across three worlds**, featuring progressively challenging level designs, enemies and obstacles.
- **Diverse movement mechanics**, including double jumping, wall jumping and gravity inversion.
- **Power-ups**, including antigravity, featherfall and enemy intimidation.
- **Interactive environments**, featuring moving platforms, checkpoints, trick blocks, lock blocks and temporary blocks.
- **Progress tracking**, including saved level progression.

## Development History

The original version of Blocko was released in 2018 and received several updates throughout 2018 and 2019. It became popular among classmates, with its challenging levels encouraging players to compete for the fastest completion times.

Following a period of inactivity, development resumed in 2024. This revival introduced new levels, additional power-ups and adjustments to the difficulty progression, particularly in the second world.

The project provided an opportunity to explore game design, develop new gameplay ideas and share an independently created game with friends and family.
